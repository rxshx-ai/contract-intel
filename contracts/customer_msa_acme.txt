MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement is entered into as of 15 April 2026 by and between Contoso Systems Ltd. ("Contoso") and Acme Financial Group plc ("Client").

1. TERM

1.1 The initial term of this Agreement is twenty-four (24) months commencing on 1 May 2026.
1.2 This Agreement shall renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless Client gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2. FEES

2.1 Client shall pay an annual subscription fee of USD 640,000, invoiced annually in advance.
2.2 Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 Availability. Contoso warrants that the Platform will be available not less than 99.99% of the time in each calendar month.
3.2 Service Credits. If availability falls below 99.99% in any calendar month, Client shall receive a service credit of fifteen percent (15%) of the monthly fee, applied automatically without requirement of a claim.
3.3 Support. Contoso shall respond to priority-one incidents within one (1) hour, twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week.

4. DATA PROTECTION

4.1 Breach Notification. Contoso shall notify Client of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of such breach.
4.2 Data Deletion. Upon termination or upon Client's written request, Contoso shall securely delete all Client Data within thirty (30) days.
4.3 Subprocessors. Contoso shall not engage any new subprocessor without providing Client thirty (30) days prior written notice and an opportunity to object.
4.4 Audit. Client may audit Contoso's information security controls once per calendar year upon thirty (30) days written notice, at Client's expense.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

5.1 Contoso's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed five million dollars ($5,000,000).
5.2 The limitation in Section 5.1 shall not apply to Contoso's indemnification obligations under Section 6 or to Contoso's breach of its data protection obligations under Section 4, which shall be unlimited.

6. INDEMNIFICATION

6.1 Contoso shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client against any third party claim alleging that the Platform infringes any intellectual property right.

7. TERMINATION

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon a material breach that remains uncured sixty (60) days after written notice.
7.2 Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days written notice.

8. GENERAL

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
8.2 Assignment. Either party may assign this Agreement to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of all or substantially all of its assets upon written notice.
8.3 Reporting. Contoso shall deliver a written service performance report to Client within ten (10) days of the end of each calendar month.
